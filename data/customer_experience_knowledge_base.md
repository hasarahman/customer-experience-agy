# Customer Experience Knowledge Base

## About Customer Experience
Customer Experience is an online retailer founded in 2016, shipping products worldwide from
our fulfillment centers in Portland, OR and Columbus, OH. We carry over 2 million titles across
books, media, textbooks, and rare/collectible editions. Customer support is available via
chat and email, Monday–Saturday, 8am–8pm ET.

## Shipping Policy

### Domestic Shipping (United States)
| Method | Cost | Delivery Time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $4.99 (free over $35) | 5–7 business days |
| Expedited | $9.99 | 2–3 business days |
| Overnight | $24.99 | 1 business day (order by 12pm ET) |

- Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day; otherwise they ship the next
  business day.
- We do not ship on weekends or federal holidays.
- P.O. boxes and APO/FPO addresses are supported for Standard shipping only.

### International Shipping
- Available to Canada, UK, EU, and Australia.
- Flat rate: $14.99, 10–18 business days.
- Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.
- International orders are not eligible for Expedited or Overnight shipping.

### Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed automatically once an order ships.
- Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the order ships.
- If a tracking number shows no movement for 5+ business days, treat it as a delayed
  shipment and offer to escalate.

### Delays
- Weather, carrier disruptions, and high-volume periods (e.g., November–December) can add
  2–4 extra business days beyond the stated window.
- Delayed orders are not automatically refunded; the shipping fee is refunded only if the
  delay exceeds 10 business days past the original estimate.

## Return & Refund Policy

- Return window: 30 days from the delivery date.
- Items must be in original condition; textbooks with excessive highlighting/writing are not
  eligible unless purchased as "used."
- **Damaged, defective, or incorrect items:** full refund or free replacement, no return
  shipping cost to the customer. Customer Experience emails a prepaid return label.
- **Change of mind / no longer wanted:** eligible for refund minus original shipping cost;
  customer pays return shipping (flat $5.99, deducted from refund).
- **Final sale items** (marked "Clearance" or "Rare/Collectible"): not eligible for return
  except if damaged or misdescribed.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the
  returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse.
- Store credit is available immediately as an alternative to a refund and never expires.
- Gift orders can be returned for store credit only, issued to the recipient.

## Payment Methods
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, and Customer Experience Gift Cards.
- Customer Experience does not support installment/buy-now-pay-later options at this time.
- Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address at checkout.

## Account & Password
- Accounts are created with an email address and password; no username required.
- Password reset is handled via a one-time verification code sent to the email or phone
  number on file (never sent as a plaintext password).
- Reset links expire after 15 minutes for security.
- Customers can update their email address only after verifying their current one.
- Accounts are automatically logged out after 90 days of inactivity for security.

## Order Status Definitions
- **Processing** – payment confirmed, order being prepared at the warehouse (0–1 business
  day).
- **Shipped** – order has left the warehouse and has a tracking number.
- **Out for Delivery** – carrier has the package on a delivery vehicle for that day.
- **Delivered** – carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- **Delayed** – tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days past the estimate.
- **Cancelled** – order was cancelled before shipping, either by the customer or Customer Experience (e.g.,
  item out of stock).

## Loyalty Program: Customer Experience Rewards
- Free to join; customers earn 1 point per $1 spent.
- 500 points = $5 off a future order.
- Members get free Standard shipping on all orders, regardless of order total.
- Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

## Common Policy Exceptions
- Pre-order titles ship on the official release date, not the order date; standard return
  window starts from the delivery date as usual.
- Digital gift cards are non-refundable once delivered by email.
- Bulk/wholesale orders (25+ units) are handled by a separate business sales team and are
  not covered by this support flow.
